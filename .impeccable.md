## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, collaborators, founders and friends landing on arjunkalbag.xyz, often from a LinkedIn/GitHub link or an iMessage preview, frequently on a phone. They want to understand in seconds who Arjun is (WashU Computer Engineering & Economics '29, NYC / St. Louis), what he has built (Pairyx, Artifice, AK Labs, genre-space, Playback, Vantage Prep…), and where to go next (Music, Projects, Photography pages, contact).

### Brand Personality
Three words: **warm, engineered, unhurried.** An engineer who also produces music and shoots 35mm film. Confident without shouting; the craft shows in the details, not in effects.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light, warm "paper" theme (beige, tinted neutrals in OKLCH, hue ~80) with warm near-black ink; one sparse cool accent (cobalt, hue ~266) for focus, selection and hover moments only. Optional warm espresso dark mode.
- Display: **Alte Haas Grotesk Bold** for the name, the AK monogram and every display title (the name used to be Inktera; changed 2026-11-09). Body/UI: **Schibsted Grotesk**. JetBrains Mono only for the ASCII splash.
- Single page, full-screen, built out: masthead → tagline/facts → drifting film strip → About → Selected work → More → Pages → Contact panel.
- No background grid, no hairline dividers, no dropdown sections: grouping comes from space, scale and surface tone.
- Signature moment: "ARJUN KALBAG" sits big in the top-left corner (cap line level with the bar's AK; on phones it sits edge to edge just under the bar). The first few px of scroll send it into the bar: one fixed copy moves and scales as a single piece (log-space scale around the cap centre, fixed 950ms cubic-bezier(.45,0,.2,1) flight that ends cleanly; no spring, whose long tail read as the AK drifting up in the bar) while the rest of the name dissolves and the K appears beside the A; back at the very top it grows out again. No letter-by-letter motion, no pin, no scroll hijacking. At rest the real text shows (name in page, AK in bar). The pill bar runs almost edge to edge (inset clamp(8px, .9vw, 14px), capped at 2000px), 14px radius, and its contents align with the page gutters. Until the tagline ("Building projects…") reaches the top, the AK and the menu float with no pill; then the pill surface fades in behind them, in place (it must never slide: a moving surface makes the AK look like it shifts inside the bar).
- The mark: a dot-matrix horse (round black dots on an 80x58 grid, dot = 84% of pitch) running in place between the name and the tagline. Torso, neck, head and mane are the traced silhouette of the user's reference horse (long arched neck, small refined head, deep chest); legs and tail are procedural so they can walk, trot, canter and gallop (four gait keyframes blended continuously by pace: leg timing, stride, lift, body rock, head nod, neck carriage, tail hang to stream). It builds in about a second like the reference GIF (one dot, a line, the horse). Cursor: dots part on quick critically-damped springs and settle exactly; a click/tap sends a ring through them. Pace: scrolling while the pointer is over the horse changes its pace (down = faster, up = slower), clamped between a walk and a gallop so it never stops and never races; the page does not scroll for that gesture (a scroll gesture belongs to whatever it started over). There is deliberately no on-screen hint or caption. Touch has no pace control (scrolling is never touched). Reduced motion = one still horse and the wheel is left alone. The Pause button pauses it too.
- Favicon / app icon: the galloping-horse silhouette (white-paper horse on a warm near-black tile, the site's ink colour), traced to a vector in favicon.svg. Tab icons have rounded corners and a slight optical nudge; apple-touch/PWA icons are full-bleed with pure geometric centring and extra margin (maskable has the 80% safe zone). The link-preview card (og-card.jpg) still shows the old AK mark.
- No parallax. Motion is slow and soft (~0.85–1.1s ease-out hovers); nothing rolls or spins. Arrow hovers fill ink (black), not the accent.
- Display titles, the name and the AK are all Alte Haas Grotesk Bold. Hero: tagline left, "Currently at Anthos Capital" (logo, display grotesk) opposite.
- Anti-references: generic dev-portfolio templates, glassmorphism everywhere, gradient text, card grids, side-stripe borders.

### Design Principles
1. The name is the hero: big in the top-left corner, and it becomes the navigation.
2. Space over lines: separate with rhythm and tone, never rules.
3. Motion is attached to the user (scroll-linked, hover-linked), eased out, and always respects reduced motion.
4. Mobile is designed, not shrunk: the name sits in the bar's row level with the AK and menu (cap line + left edge shared with the content), the hero may run past one screen (never squeeze it to fit), 22px gutters, generous row spacing, 44px targets, safe areas, no hover-only affordances.
5. Accent is rare: cobalt appears only where attention should go.
