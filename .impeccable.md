## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, collaborators, founders and friends landing on arjunkalbag.xyz, often from a LinkedIn/GitHub link or an iMessage preview, frequently on a phone. They want to understand in seconds who Arjun is (WashU Computer Engineering & Economics '29, NYC / St. Louis), what he has built (Pairyx, Artifice, AK Labs, genre-space, Playback, Vantage Prep…), and where to go next (Music, Projects, Photography pages, contact).

### Brand Personality
Three words: **warm, engineered, unhurried.** An engineer who also produces music and shoots 35mm film. Confident without shouting; the craft shows in the details, not in effects.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light, warm "paper" theme (beige, tinted neutrals in OKLCH, hue ~80) with warm near-black ink; one sparse cool accent (cobalt, hue ~266) for focus, selection and hover moments only. Optional warm espresso dark mode.
- Display: **Inktera** (the existing brand wordmark face) as a full-width masthead and for big titles. Body/UI: **Schibsted Grotesk**. JetBrains Mono only for the ASCII splash.
- Single page, full-screen, built out: masthead → tagline/facts → drifting film strip → About → Selected work → More → Pages → Contact panel.
- No background grid, no hairline dividers, no dropdown sections: grouping comes from space, scale and surface tone.
- Signature moment: the masthead name folds into the "AK" monogram, which docks inside a floating pill menu bar (14px radius, not fully round). At the top the first scroll is held: the page nudges ~24px, the name folds (~0.7s), and only then does the page scroll. Back at the top it unfolds.
- No parallax. Motion is slow and soft (~0.85–1.1s ease-out hovers); nothing rolls or spins. Arrow hovers fill ink (black), not the accent.
- Display titles in Alte Haas Grotesk Bold (Inktera only for the name + AK). Hero: tagline left, "Currently at Anthos Capital" (logo, display grotesk) opposite.
- Anti-references: generic dev-portfolio templates, glassmorphism everywhere, gradient text, card grids, side-stripe borders.

### Design Principles
1. The name is the hero: big, edge-to-edge, and it becomes the navigation.
2. Space over lines: separate with rhythm and tone, never rules.
3. Motion is attached to the user (scroll-linked, hover-linked), eased out, and always respects reduced motion.
4. Mobile is designed, not shrunk: two-line masthead, 44px targets, safe areas, no hover-only affordances.
5. Accent is rare: cobalt appears only where attention should go.
